About

Soma means to study.

In both Shona and Swahili, "soma" means to study or to learn. We built Soma Browser so that students can use the internet to do exactly that, safely.

Why we built it

The web is not made for students

The open internet is full of threats and content no student should see. Soma Browser sits between students and the web so they can focus on learning.

A shield for the open web

Soma Shield blocks threats and inappropriate sites, and security updates ship every week.

Search that puts learning first

Boomer, the default search, ranks past papers, homework help and revision material first. Students can switch to Google or another engine any time.

An AI that knows your school

Soma AI works with your school's calendars, activity and records, protected by strict privacy safeguards, so it can answer what is next and help with tutoring.

The school in one sidebar

Results, plans, events, files and video calls sit one click away. Teachers get tools to add results and run classes.

Private by design

The school never sees what a student searches or browses.

The team

Built by two people who just left school

Tanatswa Chiyangwa and Arthur Tachiona

Soma was founded by two 19-year-olds in Harare. They know the system because they just graduated from it, and they started with one question: what would make the internet safe enough for every student? Soma Browser is the answer, and it is already used by more than 10 schools in Zimbabwe.
